### Font Style Systems

| System | Fonts | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Clean & Professional | Lato, Open Sans, Helvetica Neue | Versatile, readable, clinical |
| Contemporary & Minimalist | Montserrat, Raleway, Poppins | Modern, upscale, fresh |
| Luxury & Elegant | Playfair Display, Merriweather, Libre Baskerville | High-income, image-conscious audience |
| Approachable & Humanist | Karla, Quicksand, Work Sans | Compassionate, reconstructive care |

### Brand Color Highlights

| Color | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Light Mocha | `#B89F8B` | Neutral base |
| Cream White | `#FDFCF9` | Clean section backgrounds |
| Muted Rose | `#C97A7E` | CTA buttons, subtle elegance |
| Espresso Brown | `#4B3621` | Headings, footer |
| Charcoal Black | `#2E2E2E` | Strong contrast text |
