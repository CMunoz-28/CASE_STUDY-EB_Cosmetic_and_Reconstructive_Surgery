## Project Overview

East Bay Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery sought strategic marketing support across three core areas:

1. **Rebranding** — Refresh brand identity and messaging to segment the practice into two distinct audience journeys: Reconstructive Surgery (injury, recovery) and Cosmetic Surgery (aesthetic, elective)
2. **Website Redesign** — Modernize user experience and enhance lead conversion on a new CMS
3. **Social Media Launch** — Establish brand presence on Instagram to drive awareness and engagement

---

## Deliverables & Responsibilities

### 1. Rebranding Strategy
**Lead:** Naisargi Doshi  
**Estimated Hours:** 15–20 | **Timeline:** 2 weeks

- Brand audit and competitor analysis
- Updated brand positioning and value proposition
- Messaging pillars and brand tone definition
- Revised visual style guide (colors, fonts, iconography)
- Taglines and messaging for reconstructive vs. cosmetic

### 2. Website Redesign
**Lead:** Carlos Munoz  
**Estimated Hours:** 40–50 | **Timeline:** 4–5 weeks

- Sitemap separating reconstructive and cosmetic segments
- Wireframes and user flow mapping
- Copywriting aligned with brand tone
- Desktop/mobile UX and UI development
- Before/after gallery, blog integration, and appointment CTA setup
- Technical SEO implementation (speed, keywords, metadata)
- Google Analytics and lead tracking tools setup

### 3. Social Media Marketing Setup
**Lead:** Naisargi Doshi  
**Estimated Hours:** 30–40 initial | 15–20 hrs/month ongoing | **Timeline:** 2–3 weeks

- Optimize Instagram profile (bio, Linktree, profile image)
- Social media content calendar (first 3 months)
- Content creation: carousels, reels, before/after, FAQ posts
- Hashtag strategy and caption writing
- Paid ad campaign audience planning (optional)

### 4. Lead Generation & SEO Support
**Lead:** Carlos Munoz  
**Estimated Hours:** 5–8 | **Timeline:** 1 week (overlapping with website launch)

- Keyword research for SEO and content
- Setup of HIPAA-compliant forms
- Integration of lead capture and analytics tracking
- Google My Business profile review/setup
