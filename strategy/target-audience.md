# Target Audience Analysis

**Client:** East Bay Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery  
**Framework:** Step2Growth Marketing Workbook  
**Date:** August 2025

---

## Audience Brainstorm

### Shared Identities Considered

- Young millennials focused on overall skin maintenance
- Older women looking to reduce wrinkles
- Acne-prone individuals seeking long-term skin clarity
- Image-conscious high-income professionals
- Brides, grooms, and bridal party members
- Actors, musicians, and performers who maintain a youthful appearance
- Chronic headache sufferers (Botox therapeutic use)

### Universal Demographics

| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| Income | Middle to upper class |
| Age | 25+ |
| Location | Urban or suburban neighborhoods (East Bay, Oakland, surrounding areas) |
| Gender | Primarily female, with a small but consistent male client pool |
| Mindset | Image-conscious; quality-over-price orientation |

### Core Goals & Desires

- Achieve a natural, youthful appearance without looking "overdone"
- Clear fine lines and wrinkles efficiently and with lasting results
- Maintain smooth, healthy skin free of blemishes and acne scarring long-term
- Look and feel confident at key life milestones (weddings, reunions, career milestones)

---

## Decision: Primary Target Audience

> **Image-conscious high-income females**

This audience commands the highest ability to pay, has a clearly established need, and aligns directly with the practice's cosmetic service offerings. They are motivated by natural-looking, high-quality outcomes — not discounts — which fits the positioning of a board-certified specialist over a med-spa chain.

---

## Implications for Messaging & Marketing

| Channel | Application |
|---|---|
| Website copy | Lead with outcomes, not procedures. Use "natural," "refined," "confidence" over clinical jargon |
| Instagram | Before/after imagery, patient-journey storytelling, educational carousels on non-surgical options |
| Paid ads | Target by income, zip code (East Bay), and interest signals (skincare, beauty, wellness) |
| CTAs | "Schedule a Consultation" over "Book Now" — signals a premium, personalized experience |
| Trust signals | Highlight Dr. Chin's credentials, board certifications, and Harvard/MIT/UCLA training upfront |

---

## Secondary Audience: Reconstructive Patients

While the cosmetic segment is the primary growth driver, the practice's dual specialization in reconstructive surgery (trauma, hand/upper extremity, nerve and tendon repair, scar revision) is a meaningful differentiator. This audience is driven by medical necessity rather than elective choice, and the website architecture should reflect a clear separation between these two journeys — ensuring reconstructive patients don't feel lost in a cosmetic-first experience.
