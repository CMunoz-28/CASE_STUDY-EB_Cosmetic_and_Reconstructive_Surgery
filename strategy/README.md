## Strategic Workstreams

**Rebranding** — Segment the practice into two distinct service lines (Reconstructive vs. Cosmetic), define a new brand voice, and build a visual style guide aligned with a high-income, image-conscious audience.

**Website Redesign** — Design and build a dual-audience website on WordPress with clear patient journeys, HIPAA-compliant lead capture, and technical SEO foundations.

**Social Media & Lead Generation** — Establish Instagram presence, build a content calendar, and set up Google Analytics 4 + conversion tracking.

---

## Primary ICP

> **Image-conscious, high-income females in the East Bay** — motivated by natural outcomes, trust in credentials, and a premium patient experience.
