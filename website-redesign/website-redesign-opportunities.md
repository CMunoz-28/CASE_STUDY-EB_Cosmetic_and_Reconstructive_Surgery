# Website Redesign Opportunities

**Client:** East Bay Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery  
**Audit Date:** June 28, 2025  
**Prepared by:** Carlos Munoz  
**Domain:** [bayplasticsurgery.com](http://www.bayplasticsurgery.com)  
**Pages Reviewed:** Homepage, About, Services, Contact, Sub-Service Pages  
**Tools Used:** Google PageSpeed Insights, SEO Meta in 1 Click, Screaming Frog

---

## Executive Summary

This audit assesses the current website to identify areas for improvement across technical performance, SEO, content, user experience, and lead generation. While the website presents a clean and welcoming aesthetic, it lacks key functionalities and optimizations necessary to support a dual-audience approach (reconstructive vs. cosmetic surgery) and drive conversions effectively.

**Key Findings:**
- No clear segmentation of core services by patient type
- Website load speed and mobile responsiveness need improvement
- Limited keyword optimization and weak CTA structure
- Minimal conversion tracking and analytics implementation

**Top Recommendations:**
- Redesign the homepage to reflect segmented patient journeys
- Implement on-page SEO best practices and keyword strategy
- Establish clear CTAs and lead capture flows
- Optimize technical performance (speed, mobile, alt tags)

---

## Website Design & UX

### Homepage

**Issues:**
- Repeated generic headlines ("Realize your Beauty", "Believe in your Beauty") — no differentiation
- No audience segmentation; reconstructive and cosmetic services are lumped together
- Weak visual hierarchy — generic imagery, small supporting visuals

**Recommendations:**
- Implement a split hero with two clear CTAs: **"Injury & Reconstructive Care"** and **"Cosmetic & Aesthetic Services"**
- Use visuals that resonate with each target segment (functional recovery vs. aesthetic results)
- Add bold, high-quality before/after imagery to the homepage

### Navigation & Information Architecture

**Issues:**
- Main menu is broad and undifferentiated — no service-line separation

**Recommendations:**
- Introduce a top-level split: **Reconstructive** and **Cosmetic**
- Sub-menu examples:
  - Under Cosmetic: Face, Body, Skin, Non-Surgical, Financing
  - Under Reconstructive: Trauma & Injury, Nerve & Tendon Repair, Scar Revision, Hand & Upper Extremity

### Content & Copy

**Recommendations:**
- Add concise service descriptions with outcome-driven headlines
- Inject social proof (testimonials, case highlights) early on the page
- Clearly state geographic areas served (Oakland & Greater East Bay)

## Lead Generation & Conversion

### CTAs

**Issues:**
- Prominent appointment form is good but may feel heavy for first-time visitors unfamiliar with medical consultations

**Recommendations:**
- Offer multiple CTA options:
  - "Request a Consultation"
  - "Download Patient Guide"
  - "Check Insurance Coverage"
- Create a "Reconstructive Care Interest Form" with simplified fields to reduce friction
- Ensure all forms are HIPAA-compliant and mobile-optimized

### Trust & Credibility Signals

**Issues:**
- No visible certifications or affiliations on the homepage
- No "Meet the Doctor" snippet above the fold

**Recommendations:**
- Display trust badges: "Board-Certified Surgeon," "ABPS Member," "Harvard Medical School," etc.
- Include a prominent "Meet Dr. Chin" section with photo, credentials, and a brief video or quote
- Add patient testimonials and star ratings early in the page flow

## SEO & Technical Performance

### On-Page SEO

**Issues:**
- Page titles, meta descriptions, and alt attributes are not optimized or segmented

**Recommendations:**
- Title tag example: *"Oakland Reconstructive & Cosmetic Surgery – East Bay Plastic"*
- Meta descriptions should include service-specific phrases:
  - Reconstructive: "Expert care for injury recovery and reconstructive surgery in Oakland, CA"
  - Cosmetic: "Board-certified plastic surgery for natural, lasting aesthetic results in the East Bay"

### Keyword Strategy

**Recommendations:**
- Target local search terms: "Oakland plastic surgery," "East Bay cosmetic surgeon," "Oakland reconstructive hand surgery"
- Conduct formal keyword research (SEMrush, Ahrefs) for low-competition, high-converting phrases
- Apply keywords naturally to H1/H2 headings and service page copy

### Technical Performance

**Recommendations:**
- Compress images and serve in modern formats (WebP)
- Minimize CSS/JavaScript bloat
- Ensure mobile-first responsive design
- Add ARIA labels and alt text for accessibility compliance

### Analytics & Tracking

**Issues:**
- No clear analytics scripts detected — conversion tracking likely absent

**Recommendations:**
- Install Google Analytics 4 + Google Tag Manager
- Set up conversion tracking on forms and CTA clicks
- Implement heatmaps (Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity) to identify drop-off points
- Create a monthly reporting dashboard (traffic, leads, form completions)

## CMS Platform Recommendation

**Recommended Platform: WordPress**

| Factor | Rationale |
|---|---|
| SEO control | Full access to URL structure, metadata, schema markup |
| HIPAA compliance | Integration with HIPAA-compliant form plugins (Jotform, Gravity Forms) |
| Scalability | Page builder flexibility for dual-audience architecture |
| Blog/content | Native blogging for SEO content strategy |
| Cost | Open-source; lower long-term cost than proprietary CMS |
