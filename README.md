# East Bay Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery

**Digital Strategy & Website Transformation Case Study**

---

## Context

This project was completed as part of my work as a **Marketing Advisor at the [NorCal Small Business Development Center (SBDC)](https://norcalsbdc.org/)**, where I provide no-cost, confidential marketing advising to small business owners across the East Bay. The SBDC is a federally funded program supporting small business growth through advising, education, and resources.

This engagement represents the full scope of a multi-session consulting project — from initial discovery through strategy, competitive research, design, and migration planning.

---

## Project Overview

**Client:** [East Bay Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery](https://www.bayplasticsurgery.com/) — a medical practice in Oakland, CA offering both reconstructive and cosmetic surgery under Dr. Douglas Chin, a board-certified plastic surgeon trained at Harvard, MIT, NYU, and UCLA.

**Scope:** Brand repositioning, website redesign, SEO remediation, and HIPAA-aware lead generation planning — with an emphasis on creating a scalable digital foundation that serves both insurance-based reconstructive patients and cash-pay cosmetic clients.

---

## Project Objectives

- Segment the practice into two clearly defined service lines: **Reconstructive Surgery** and **Cosmetic Surgery**
- Migrate content from a legacy website to a modern, SEO-optimized CMS (WordPress)
- Improve technical SEO health and on-site user experience
- Design HIPAA-compliant lead capture and consultation workflows
- Deliver a complete strategy, design, and migration documentation package

## My Role & Contributions

I led the **strategy, analysis, UX, and documentation** for this engagement through the NorCal SBDC, including:

- Conducted a **full content inventory & technical SEO audit** (137+ indexed pages, Screaming Frog)
- Developed a **competitor analysis** across four regional practices — positioning, digital presence, and SWOT
- Defined the **primary ICP** (image-conscious, high-income females in the East Bay) using a structured audience scoring framework
- Wrote a **Scope of Work** covering rebranding, website redesign, and social media launch (~90–118 total hours)
- Evaluated CMS platforms and recommended **WordPress** for SEO control and HIPAA plugin support
- Designed **desktop and mobile wireframes** in Figma across four iterative rounds
- Built a **visual sitemap** reflecting the dual-audience information architecture
- Developed **six logo redesign concepts** and a full color palette + typography system
- Planned content migration with URL redirect strategy to preserve domain authority
- Built project trackers, implementation timelines, and client-ready reports

## Project Impact

- 85% of legacy content successfully inventoried and mapped for migration
- Clear dual-audience architecture established (Reconstructive / Cosmetic split)
- WordPress selected as CMS — supporting long-term SEO growth and HIPAA compliance
- Technical SEO foundation documented: metadata, schema recommendations, redirect mapping
- Practice positioned to compete effectively against regional surgical and med-spa competitors
