# Content Migration

This folder documents the content audit and migration planning for East Bay Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery's website transition from the legacy site to the redesigned WordPress build.

---

## Documents

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| [`Content Inventory.xlsx`](Content%20Inventory.xlsx) | Full inventory of 137+ indexed pages — content type, migration status, SEO notes, and redirect mapping |

---

## Migration Scope

The legacy site at [bayplasticsurgery.com](https://www.bayplasticsurgery.com) had over 137 indexed pages across:

- Homepage and primary navigation pages
- Individual service pages (cosmetic and reconstructive)
- About / Meet the Doctor
- Patient resources and FAQ
- Contact and appointment pages

**Migration priorities:**
1. Preserve and redirect high-authority URLs to avoid SEO loss
2. Rewrite copy aligned with the new dual-audience brand voice
3. Update metadata (title tags, meta descriptions, alt text) per SEO audit findings
4. Consolidate thin or duplicate content into stronger, segmented service pages

---

## Outcome

- 85% of legacy content successfully mapped and migrated
- URL redirect structure documented to preserve existing domain authority
- Page-level SEO metadata refreshed across all primary service pages
